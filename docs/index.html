Safe by Design

No blind "one-click clean." Every deletion is explained and classified.

Most Mac cleaners hand you one giant number and a single button. CleanBit shows three clear tiers and lets you decide.

Recommended

Caches that rebuild themselves

App caches, browser caches (never touching cookies, history, or passwords), Xcode DerivedData, and package manager caches. Deleted permanently by default so space returns immediately—or send them to Trash in Settings.

Review

Not pre-selected. Always goes to Trash.

Project build folders (node_modules, target, .venv), old installers (.dmg, .pkg), uninstalled app leftovers, AI sessions, and uninstalled apps. Always moved to the Trash so you can put them back.

Info Only

Shown for awareness. Never deletable.

Local AI models (Hugging Face, Ollama, LM Studio), pnpm store (hard-linked), and Docker Desktop disk images. CleanBit shows how much space they take and points you to the right command.

FAQ

Common questions

Why is CleanBit only on the Mac App Store and sandboxed?

Running inside Apple’s App Sandbox means CleanBit never asks for root access, never installs a persistent background helper daemon, and can only read folders you explicitly grant in a standard macOS open panel. Full Disk Access is optional and only needed for the Trash, Safari caches, and other apps’ containers.

Can deleted items be recovered?

It depends on the risk tier, and CleanBit tells you before you confirm. Recommended caches that apps rebuild automatically are deleted permanently by default (you can switch them to Trash in Settings). Review items, build artifacts, manually picked files, and uninstalled apps always move to the Trash so you can put them back.

Why doesn’t CleanBit clean APFS local snapshots or system caches?

macOS protects system locations and APFS snapshots outside the App Sandbox. Rather than asking for root privileges or claiming to clean things macOS manages itself, CleanBit focuses strictly on the user-space caches, developer files, and leftovers inside your home folder.

Does CleanBit collect or upload my file names?

Never. Scanning, duplicate hashing, and cleaning happen entirely locally on your Mac. File names, paths, contents, and installed app lists never leave your device. CleanBit uses Firebase Analytics only for anonymous product telemetry (built-in rule IDs, counts, and sizes rounded to MB).

What macOS version is required?

CleanBit is built natively with SwiftUI for macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later.
