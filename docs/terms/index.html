3. Cleaning, Deletion, and Your Responsibility#

CleanBit deletes files on your Mac. Please read this section carefully.

  • You decide what is deleted. CleanBit labels each item as Recommended (pre-selected), Review (not pre-selected), or Info Only (never deletable), and shows what each category contains and the impact of removing it. Review the selection before you confirm a clean.
  • How items are removed. By default, Recommended items are deleted permanently, and a setting lets you send them to the Trash instead. Review items, files you pick by hand, and uninstalled apps are always moved to the Trash. Items already in the Trash can only be deleted permanently. Permanently deleted items cannot be recovered through CleanBit.
  • Side effects. Removing caches, build artifacts, or app data may cause apps to rebuild data, re-download content, take longer to start, or sign you out. Removing AI tool sessions, project artifacts, or app leftovers may delete history or settings you still want.
  • Back up your data. Keep current backups, such as Time Machine, before cleaning. We are not responsible for data you choose to delete, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
  • Dry Run Mode. When Dry Run Mode is on, CleanBit only simulates a clean and deletes nothing.
