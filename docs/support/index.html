CleanBit Support#

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Welcome to CleanBit Support. We’re here to help with technical issues, purchase questions, general feedback, and feature requests.

Contact Support#

Email: support@pokiai.com

When contacting us, please include:

  • your macOS version;
  • your CleanBit version;
  • a clear description of the issue;
  • steps to reproduce the issue, if possible; and
  • screenshots or a screen recording, if applicable.

Scan results can show file names and folder paths. Please check screenshots before sending them, and do not include passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive personal information.

Quick Troubleshooting#

CleanBit cannot scan my Mac#

  • CleanBit runs in the macOS App Sandbox and needs you to grant access to your home folder. Open Settings → Access in CleanBit and grant access again, choosing your home folder in the panel.
  • Some items, such as the Trash and caches inside other apps' containers, need Full Disk Access. Turn on CleanBit in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, then return to CleanBit.
  • Uninstalling apps needs access to the Applications folder. CleanBit asks for it on the Uninstall Apps page, or you can grant it in Settings → Access.

An item is shown but cannot be selected#

CleanBit blocks items that are unsafe to remove at the moment, for example caches of an app that is currently running or tools that were used very recently. The reason is shown next to the item. Quit the related app and scan again. Items labeled Info Only are shown for information and can never be deleted.

Less space was freed than expected#

  • Items moved to the Trash still take space until you empty the Trash.
  • macOS may keep space in use for local snapshots and purgeable data, and frees it over time.
  • Some apps recreate their caches as soon as they run again.

I deleted something by mistake#

  • Items moved to the Trash can be put back from the Trash in Finder.
  • Items deleted permanently cannot be recovered by CleanBit. Restore them from a backup such as Time Machine.
  • The History page lists every clean and how each item was removed.
  • To try CleanBit without deleting anything, turn on Dry Run Mode in Settings → General. To send Recommended items to the Trash instead of deleting them permanently, turn off Delete “Recommended” caches permanently in the same section.

Purchase or restore is not working#

  • Confirm that you are signed in to the Mac App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • Reopen CleanBit and choose Restore Purchases in Settings → Purchase.
  • To manage or cancel a subscription, open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings → Subscriptions.
  • If the purchase was charged but cleaning is still limited, contact us and describe what happened. Do not send payment-card information.

CleanBit is not behaving as expected#

Quit and reopen the App. If the issue remains, open Settings → Diagnostics, run the checks, and contact us with the results and the steps that reproduce the problem.
